\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Very small sample, no statistics.} Five issues, one repository, mostly one run per cell and five seeded pull requests with two or three trials each. Differences between variants are described, not tested, and a single lucky or unlucky run can flip a cell. The same person wrote the issues, the hidden tests, the seeded defects and the workflow.
\item \textbf{Free hosted models that change under us.} All models are free preview endpoints with daily token quotas and variable latency. Runs were spread over several days, models were revised five times (\adr{006}), and sampling settings (temperature, seeds) are not pinned. The builders are small open models; the numbers say little about frontier models, and a rerun on another day can differ.
\item \textbf{The workflow was developed on the evaluation issue.} Gates, prompts and routing rules were tuned on runs of issue \#7 (and on the artifacts of those runs). Results for that issue are therefore not blind, and the other issues are the fairer test. Every failed attempt is kept and listed in \code{results/runs.md}.
\item \textbf{Hidden tests encode our reading of the issues.} Issue \#8 is vague on purpose and the tests accept either of two readings; issue \#5's natural fix conflicts with an existing test that locks in the old behaviour. A different but reasonable implementation can fail a test, and a test can pass for the wrong reason.
\item \textbf{Reviewer independence is partial.} The reviewer is a different vendor from the builders, but its first two tiers are both NVIDIA models, its third tier equals a model tried as tester, and the builders are all from one family. A reviewer that fails (prose answer, no evidence, budget) is recorded as \emph{no review}, which is safe but lowers its measured catch rate.
\item \textbf{The planner sees a trimmed architecture note} (\adr{011}); we did not test whether the full note would have produced better designs.
\item \textbf{Incomplete token accounting.} Per-call token logging was added after the first runs, and local development runs are not counted; the total reported in the appendix is therefore a lower bound.
\end{enumerate}
